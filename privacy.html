Privacy Policy

Usage Tracker for Claude · Last updated 16 November 2026

Usage Tracker for Claude shows how much of your Claude plan's usage limits you have used. It runs entirely on your device. There is no backend, account or proxy run by the developer.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Your claude.ai sign-in

When you sign in, you do so on claude.ai's own page inside the app. The app keeps the resulting session cookies in the Keychain on your device and uses them only to fetch your usage and activity figures from claude.ai. Your password is never seen or stored by the app.

Data that stays on your device

Your latest usage figures, activity statistics and settings are stored on your device and shared only with the app's own widgets and Live Activity. Notifications are scheduled locally. Signing out or deleting the app removes this data.

Who the app talks to

The app connects only to claude.ai and api.anthropic.com, directly from your device, to read your own usage. Those requests are covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

Sample data

"Try it with sample data" shows made-up figures and makes no network requests.
