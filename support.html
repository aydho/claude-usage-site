Support

Usage Tracker for Claude shows your Claude plan's session and weekly limits on iPhone, iPad and the Mac menu bar.

Contact

For help, bug reports or feature requests, email aydh@aydho.com.

Getting started

Open the app and tap Sign in to claude.ai. Sign in with your email address or Apple account; Google sign-in is blocked inside apps, so use Continue with email if you normally use Google. To look around first, tap Try it with sample data.

Common questions

Which plans does it work with? Any claude.ai plan with usage limits, such as Pro and Max.

Why does it say claude.ai asked for a browser check? Open claude.ai from the app's Settings once, then refresh.

How do I add the widget? Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Claude Usage. On a Mac, use Edit Widgets in Notification Centre.

How often does it update? Every few minutes while the app runs; choose the interval in Settings. iOS decides how often widgets refresh in the background.
